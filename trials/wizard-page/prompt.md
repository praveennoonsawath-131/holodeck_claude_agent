Create a new page called "Wizard" that guides the user through a three-step sign-up wizard. It should have:

- A page heading "Sign-up Wizard".
- A step indicator showing the three steps in order: "Account", "Profile" and "Review", with the current step highlighted.
- Step 1 "Account": an Email field and a Password field.
- Step 2 "Profile": a Full name field and a Country dropdown.
- Step 3 "Review": a read-only summary of the values entered in the earlier steps.
- Navigation buttons: "Back" (hidden on the first step), "Next" on steps 1 and 2, and "Submit" on the last step. "Next" must not move on while a required field of the current step is empty.

Use the standard WaveMaker UI components and keep the layout clean and responsive.
